FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before investing in solar. Still have questions? Our consultants are a call away.

Ask an Expert →
How does solar panel installation work? +
Our process begins with a site assessment and energy audit. We then design a custom system, handle all permitting, and install the panels with certified engineers. After commissioning, we test the full system and connect your remote monitoring dashboard.
How long does installation take? +
Most residential systems are completed in 1–3 days. Larger commercial projects may take 1–2 weeks depending on system size and site complexity. We always provide a clear timeline before work begins.
What are the benefits of going solar? +
Significant reduction in electricity bills, independence from grid price increases, low carbon footprint, increased property value, and — in Saudi Arabia's climate — exceptionally high energy yield year-round.
How long do solar panels last? +
Quality panels like our JA Solar range are rated for 25–30 years. They degrade minimally over time — typically less than 0.7% per year — so you'll still have strong output well into the third decade.
Do solar panels require maintenance? +
Solar panels are largely maintenance-free. We recommend periodic cleaning (especially in dusty regions) and an annual inspection. Our maintenance packages cover all of this, plus remote monitoring to catch any performance issues early.
Can solar panels work on cloudy or dusty days? +
Yes — modern panels still generate power in diffuse light conditions. Saudi Arabia's high solar irradiance means even on hazy days, output remains well above global averages. Battery storage options can further smooth out any variability.
